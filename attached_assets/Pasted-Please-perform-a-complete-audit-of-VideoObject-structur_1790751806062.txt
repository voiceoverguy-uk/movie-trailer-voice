Please perform a complete audit of VideoObject structured data across the MovieTrailerVoice website:

https://www.movietrailervoice.co.uk/

IMPORTANT: This is an AUDIT / PLAN only. Do not make any code changes yet.

Use Google's CURRENT Video structured data guidance as the primary standard:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video

This website contains a substantial amount of video content, so please inspect the actual codebase, shared components/templates and page-specific implementations rather than checking only one or two pages.

GOAL

I want to ensure every VideoObject is truthful, technically correct, consistent with the visible video, and compliant with Google's current guidance.

Please identify every place VideoObject structured data is generated or rendered and audit all published VideoObjects.

For each VideoObject, check:

- `@type`
- `@id`
- `name`
- `description`
- `thumbnailUrl`
- `uploadDate`
- `contentUrl`
- `embedUrl`
- `duration`
- `creator`
- `author`
- `interactionStatistic`
- any other video-specific properties currently being generated

VISIBLE VIDEO MATCHING

Confirm that every VideoObject corresponds to an actual video visibly available on that page.

Check that:

- the schema title identifies the same video the visitor sees;
- description matches the actual video;
- thumbnail belongs to that video;
- player/embed URL belongs to that video;
- there is no VideoObject markup for audio-only content;
- there is no markup for videos merely linked from the page but not actually available on it;
- the same video is not accidentally represented by conflicting duplicate VideoObjects.

YOUTUBE / VIDEO PROVIDER CHECK

Where videos are hosted by YouTube or another external provider:

- verify that the video ID in the schema matches the visible embedded video;
- verify that thumbnail URLs genuinely exist;
- do NOT assume `maxresdefault.jpg` exists for every YouTube video;
- identify broken or unavailable thumbnails;
- check that `embedUrl` and/or `contentUrl` are being used appropriately;
- account for any consent-gated or lazy-loaded video embeds.

UPLOAD DATE — IMPORTANT

Audit every `uploadDate`.

The date must represent the genuine publication/upload date of the VIDEO where that can be established.

Do NOT accept:
- the surrounding web page publication date;
- blog/article date;
- page modification date;
- build/deployment date;
- today's date;
- a hard-coded fallback date

unless it genuinely represents the video's publication date.

Where the genuine video publication date cannot be reliably established, flag that individual VideoObject for owner confirmation.

Do not recommend inventing or estimating a date simply to satisfy Google's required fields.

CREATOR / AUTHOR

Review Google's current guidance concerning `creator` and `author`.

Determine what entity should truthfully receive creator credit for the videos on MovieTrailerVoice.

Where the site already contains a stable Person, Organization, LocalBusiness or other relevant entity with an `@id`, recommend reusing that entity rather than generating disconnected duplicates.

Do not automatically assume every video was personally created by Guy Harris simply because he performs the voiceover.

Distinguish appropriately between:
- voiceover performer;
- video creator;
- video publisher;
- production company/client;
- MovieTrailerVoice/VoiceoverGuy.

If the evidence does not establish who actually created/published a video, flag it rather than guessing.

Recommend `creator` OR `author` where appropriate rather than automatically duplicating both.

INTERACTION STATISTICS

If `interactionStatistic` is currently used:

- verify that the interaction type is supported by Google's current guidance;
- verify that any figures are genuine and maintainable.

Do not invent:
- views;
- likes;
- engagement;
- duration;
- interaction counts.

If reliable interaction data is unavailable, advise whether the property should simply be omitted.

DURATION

If duration is present:
- verify that it represents the actual video duration;
- verify ISO 8601 formatting;
- flag any generated/default/fabricated durations.

ENTITY / GRAPH CONSISTENCY

Check whether VideoObjects are correctly connected to the site's existing schema graph.

Look for:
- duplicate Person entities;
- duplicate Organization/LocalBusiness entities;
- conflicting `@id` values;
- orphaned VideoObjects;
- duplicated VideoObjects for the same media;
- inconsistent domain/canonical URLs;
- video entities that could reference existing site entities more cleanly.

Do NOT create new entities merely to make the schema look more elaborate.

GOOGLE ELIGIBILITY VS SCHEMA.ORG VALIDITY

Please distinguish between:

1. technically valid Schema.org markup;
2. Google's required/recommended VideoObject properties;
3. markup that is technically valid but unlikely to qualify for Google's video features;
4. demonstrably inaccurate structured data.

Accuracy is more important than filling every possible property.

If a video does not have enough reliable information for a truthful Google-eligible VideoObject, recommend omitting/defering its VideoObject rather than fabricating missing data.

SITE-WIDE ARCHITECTURE

Because MovieTrailerVoice contains many videos, also assess whether VideoObject markup is being generated efficiently.

Please determine:
- whether video schema comes from a shared component/helper;
- whether individual pages hard-code their own JSON-LD;
- whether there are inconsistent implementations across the site;
- whether one central video-data structure/helper would make the schema safer and easier to maintain;
- whether changing a shared component could unintentionally affect many pages.

Do not recommend a major refactor unless there is a genuine maintainability or accuracy benefit.

VALIDATION

Please plan to test representative rendered pages against:
- Google's Rich Results Test where applicable;
- Schema.org Validator;
- actual live/rendered JSON-LD output.

Do not rely solely on TypeScript/build success as proof that structured data is correct.
